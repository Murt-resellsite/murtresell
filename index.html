📲 Get the App

Put StreamPack on your home screen. It opens full-screen like a normal app. Takes 10 seconds:

  1. 1
    In Safari, tap the Share button (bottom of the screen).
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3
    Tap Add. Open StreamPack from your home screen and log in once.

Not in Safari? Open this page in Safari first. The app updates by itself whenever you upload a new version to GitHub.

📋 Packing List

🏷️ Tag Brands on Existing Items

This only sets the brand — it never changes quantity. Every fragrance item that doesn't have a brand yet is listed below; just type the brand after each "=". Leave a line's brand blank to skip it.

📥 Bulk Add Items

One item per line. Paste from an invoice or type them out — format is flexible, but line up fields with commas like this:

Name, Qty, Size, Cost, Brand

Size, Cost, and Brand are optional — leave them blank or skip trailing fields entirely. Brand is only used for Fragrances.

⇄ Swap Item

Pick what this should become instead. The original goes back into inventory automatically.

🎁 Make a Bundle

Tap everything that goes with (pick as many as you want). Everything in the bundle comes out of inventory and becomes one bundle you can sell with one tap.

📁 Past Shows

Each show is saved individually. Tap a show to view its packing list.

🛟 Local Backups

These are automatic snapshots of your show history saved on this device, most recent first. If a show ever goes missing, find a snapshot from before it disappeared and restore it — this also repairs your cloud sync.

✏️ Edit Item

Changes save as you go.

💾 Backup

Save a copy of your inventory, or load one back in.

Export saves your inventory as a file you can back up or move to another device.
Import loads a previously exported file — this will replace your current inventory.
